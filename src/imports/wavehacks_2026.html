A Ecosystem is Forming

First editions are remembered by the students who competed, the institutions that showed up, and the investor community watching a region come alive.

The Opportunity

India produces 1.5 million engineering graduates every year. Most come from colleges in smaller cities—Kasaragod, Mangalore, Kozhikode—where technical ability is high and opportunity is nearly absent. No start-ups culture. No mentors. No room to build.

Coastal Innovation Hackathon changes that equation. 24 hours. A real judging panel. Rs. 1,50,000 on the line.

Top teams walk away with prize money and a confirmed invitation to pitch at the Coastal Innovation Summit—the same room as VC and angel investors from Bangalore, Kochi, and Mangalore.

The Organizers

The institutions and communities powering the next generation of coastal builders.

BuildUp Kasaragod

A registered non-profit society building the startup ecosystem between North Malabar and South Canara. Working at the intersection of student talent, institutional infrastructure, and the investor community.

Hosts of the Coastal Innovation Summit

Sahyadri College of Engineering

Venue and Co-Organising Partner under a formal MoU. Selected twice as a Smart India Hackathon Nodal Centre by the Ministry of Education and AICTE (2022 and 2024).

Hosted 27 teams across India for SIH 2024

Community Partner

SOSC

Sahyadri Open Source Community. A student-led open-source and technology community focused on building technical skills through hands-on learning, collaboration, and peer-driven innovation with an active network of 100+ members.
